Privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Echo Lap is published by Ram C (“we”). Contact: support@numfall.store.

The short version

Echo Lap collects no data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us.

What is stored, and where

  • Your progress and settings (stars, best times, Sparks, the racers and styles you own, your settings) are saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage.
  • Best-run ghosts (the path of your fastest run on a track, so you can race it) are saved only on your device.
  • We never ask for your name, age, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Apple’s Game Center, Echo Lap reports your achievements and leaderboard scores (career stars, daily and weekly track times, survival laps) to Game Center. This is Apple’s service and is covered by Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive your Game Center identity or any other data from it. You can play without Game Center: sign out in the Settings app.

Children

Echo Lap is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children.

Deleting your data

In the game: Settings → Reset all progress. Deleting the app also deletes everything it saved on your device. Game Center scores are managed by Apple in your Game Center settings.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example if a future version adds purchases), we will update this page and the date above before the new version is released.
