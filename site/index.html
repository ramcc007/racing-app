Hold the turn. Drift. Let go.

Echo Lap is a one-thumb drift racer for iPhone and iPad. Beat each world's rival, then beat yourself: every best run comes back as an echo car. Slipstream behind your echoes to charge a boost, park one on a switch to open a shortcut, swap places with one, or dodge them when the track gets crowded.

  • 120 tracks in 8 worlds
  • A new daily track
  • Weekly gauntlet
  • Echo Survival
  • Plays offline
  • No ads, no purchases
