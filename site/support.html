Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email support@numfall.store and we’ll reply within two working days. Please mention your device and iOS version.

Controls

  • Drive: touch and hold anywhere. Lift your thumb to slow down.
  • Steer: slide left or right from where you first touched. Sensitivity is in Settings.
  • Boost: swipe up, tap with a second finger or press the boost button.
  • Relay swap (World 5 on): double-tap or press the swap button.
  • Prefer tilting? Turn on Tilt to steer in Settings. Game controllers work too.

Echoes

Every lap you finish keeps driving as an echo car with its lap number. Drive right behind one to slipstream and charge your boost. From World 2, echoes are solid: bumping one spins you out.

Stars, medals and Sparks

  • 1 star for finishing, 2 for beating the silver time, 3 for beating gold. Stars open new worlds and racers.
  • Each track has a medal goal that uses your echoes.
  • Sparks come from stars, medals, daily goals, streaks and levels. Spend them on paints, echo trails and finish celebrations in the garage.

Daily track, weekly gauntlet and Echo Survival

A new daily track appears at midnight (your time), the same for everyone. The weekly gauntlet (three races, total time counts) opens at 20 stars and changes every Monday. Echo Survival opens after track 1-5.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you are signed in, leaderboards and achievements appear in Trophies. If nothing shows, check Settings → Game Center on your device.

Motion and comfort

Turn on Calm effects in Settings (or Reduce Motion in iOS) to stop the camera rotating and shaking and to turn off particles.

Lost progress?

Progress is saved on the device only. Deleting the app deletes it.
