## Description

Every lap you drive stays on the track.

Finish a lap and it keeps racing beside you as an echo: a car that drives your exact line, lap after lap. Use your
echoes. Tuck in behind one to slipstream and charge your boost. Park one on a switch so the gate stays open on your
next lap. Swap places with the echo ahead. Or just try not to crash into the mistakes you made two laps ago.

ONE THUMB, REAL DEPTH
• Hold anywhere to drive, slide to steer, swipe up to boost.
• Easy to start, with plenty to master: racing lines, slipstream chains, perfectly timed shortcuts.

120 TRACKS IN 8 WORLDS
• Harbour, Red Canyon, Neon City, Glacier, Jungle, Foundry, Sky Port and Orbit.
• Each world adds one new rule that works with your echoes: sand, echo pads, thin ice that cracks under every
  car, relay swaps, sensor bridges, crosswinds and low-gravity jumps.
• Every track was test-driven by a robot driver before it shipped, so every gold time can be beaten.

ALWAYS SOMETHING TO CHASE
• Three stars and an echo medal on every track.
• A new daily track every day, the same for everyone, with three daily goals and a streak.
• A weekly gauntlet: three tracks back to back.
• Echo Survival: laps never end and every lap adds another echo. How long can you last?
• Race your own best-run ghost.
• Six racers to unlock, and paints, echo trails and finish celebrations to earn.
• Game Center leaderboards and 36 achievements.

FAIR AND FRIENDLY
• Free, with no ads and nothing to buy.
• No timers, no energy, no loot boxes.
• Plays fully offline. No account needed.
• Colour-blind-safe echo colours with lap numbers, Reduce Motion support, adjustable steering, tilt steering and
  game controller support.

## App Review notes (paste into "Notes")

Echo Lap is a top-down racing game built around one idea: every lap you finish stays on the track as an "echo"
car that replays your exact line. Echoes are tools: you slipstream behind them to charge boost, park them on a
switch so a shortcut gate stays open, and (from World 5) swap places with them.

- No account, sign-in, purchases, ads or tracking. The game is fully offline. Game Center is optional.
- First launch: tap "Start racing". The first race (track 1-1) teaches the controls with hints.
  Controls: hold anywhere to drive, slide left/right to steer, swipe up (or the BOOST button) to boost.
- After the first race the menu opens: Daily track, Echo Survival (opens after track 1-5), Weekly gauntlet (opens at
  20 stars), the eight worlds, Garage, Trophies and Settings.
- Later worlds unlock with stars earned by beating the target times; nothing is hidden and nothing is enabled
  remotely. A video of the later worlds is linked below.
- Settings → "Replay the first race" shows the tutorial again. Settings → "Reset all progress" returns to first launch.
